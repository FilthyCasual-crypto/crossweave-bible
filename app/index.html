Crossweave

The Bible references itself almost everywhere.

Crossweave is a reader built on that fact. Every verse is a doorway into the passages that reach into it, every place name opens onto a map, and the trail across the top keeps the path you followed in view.

The whole canon, wired together

Every chapter of scripture sits along the bottom in order, Genesis at the left and Revelation at the right, each bar as tall as the chapter is long. Every arc above is a cross-reference running between two chapters. Red arcs cross between the Testaments.

Hover a chapter to isolate its own threads. Click one to open it in the reader. Arc brightness follows reference strength, so the densest seams — Isaiah into the Gospels, Psalms into Hebrews, Exodus into Revelation — surface on their own.
